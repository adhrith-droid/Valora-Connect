Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you enter the site, such as your name and date of birth. This information is stored temporarily in your browser's session storage and is not saved to any database.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to verify you meet the minimum age requirement (18+) and to display your name during video chat sessions.

3. Video and Audio Data

VALORA uses WebRTC technology for peer-to-peer video and audio communication. We do not store or record your video calls on our servers. The connection is established directly between you and the other user.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Since we do not store your data persistently, the risk of data breaches is significantly minimized.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@valora.live.
