Valora Community Rules

Our goal is to build an enjoyable, spontaneous, and safe environment for real-time video connections. To ensure the safety of every user, strict adherence to these rules is required.

The Golden Principle: Respect & Consent

Always treat strangers on Valora with empathy and respect. Any non-consensual act, harassment, or offensive behavior will lead to an immediate, non-negotiable ban from the network.

Strictly Prohibited (Zero Tolerance)

✕

Nudity & Sexual Content

Displaying nudity, sexual acts, lingerie modeling, or unsolicited provocative exposure on camera is prohibited and results in an automatic permanent IP ban.

✕

Minors on Platform (Under 18)

Valora is strictly for adults (18+). Minors are forbidden. Promoting or facilitating child exploitation will be reported directly to international law enforcement.

✕

Harassment, Hate Speech & Bullying

Racism, bigotry, threats of physical violence, sexual harassment, discriminatory slurs, or targeted intimidation will not be tolerated.

✕

Unauthorized Recording & Doxxing

Recording, screenshotting, or streaming video conversations without the other user's explicit consent, or publishing private identities (doxxing), is forbidden.

✕

Spamming, Bots & Scams

Automated matchmaking scripts, advertising links, fake webcam feeds, cryptocurrency scams, or phishing attempts are banned instantly.

Encouraged Best Practices

✓

Friendly & Engaging Conversations

Bring positive energy, cultural curiosity, and respectful dialogue to every conversation with strangers around the globe.

✓

Respect Partner Preferences

If someone does not want to discuss a particular topic or wishes to end the call, accept it gracefully. You can easily click "Next".

✓

Clear Lighting & Working Camera

Ensure your face is visible, your microphone audio is clear, and your room background maintains your personal privacy.

✓

Help Keep the Community Safe

Use the 1-click in-call Report tool whenever you encounter violations to help our safety systems clean the network.

✓

Protect Your Identity

Keep your social handles, phone numbers, and financial details off camera until you have established mutual trust.

Moderation & Penalties

Enforcement System

We enforce a multi-tier disciplinary structure depending on the severity of the violation.

Tier 1 • Caution / Disconnect

Session Disconnect & Warning

Applied for minor infractions, uncooperative behavior, or accidental camera misdirection. The user is disconnected from matching and issued a formal guideline warning.

Tier 2 • Suspension

24 to 72 Hour Match Ban

Applied for aggressive spamming, abusive text messages, repeated skipping disruption, or vulgar comments. Access to WebRTC matching is revoked temporarily.

Tier 3 • Permanent Blacklist

Permanent IP & Hardware Ban

Applied for nudity, hate speech, threats of violence, under-18 violations, or scamming. The client IP and browser identifiers are blacklisted with zero tolerance.
