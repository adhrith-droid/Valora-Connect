Security Architecture

How We Protect You

We combine cutting-edge browser cryptography, non-persistent architectures, and rapid moderation enforcement.

Peer-to-Peer Encryption

Media streams travel directly from your device to your chat partner using standard DTLS/SRTP WebRTC protocols. Intermediary servers only facilitate the initial handshake.

Zero Call Recording

We do not capture, log, or store video feeds or microphone audio on cloud storage. Once a call is completed or skipped, live stream data ceases to exist.

Instant Report & Block

If another user violates community standards or displays offensive behavior, the in-call Report button immediately breaks the WebRTC link and queues the account for enforcement.

Automated IP Sanctions

Users who violate safety policies face immediate hardware fingerprinting and network IP blacklisting to prevent re-entry into the public matching pool.

No Phone / Social Exposure

You can chat freely without exposing phone numbers, personal social handles, or geolocation. Anonymous guest & verified single sign-on options keep your identity private.

18+ Age Protection Policy

Valora strictly prohibits minors from entering random video chat sessions. We employ age-gating checks and report escalation to ensure platform safety.

Safety Recommendations

Tips for Safe Video Chatting

Keep these best practices in mind during every stranger conversation.

✓

Never share sensitive personal info

Keep your full name, phone number, home address, workplace, and financial credentials private.

✓

Be aware of your background

Check what is visible behind you on camera (mail, diplomas, personal photos, reflection in mirrors).

✓

Skip when you feel uncomfortable

You are never obligated to stay in a call. Press the Next (Esc) button whenever you wish to switch partners.

✓

Report violations immediately

Tap the Report button during active calls to flag bad actors so our moderation system can ban them.

Incident Resolution

How Our Reporting System Works

When you flag another user, our platform takes fast automated and manual steps.

Step 01

Instant Call Disconnection

Clicking Report cuts the live WebRTC stream immediately, disconnecting the violator from your feed.

Step 02

Moderation Review & Triage

The report along with incident category and network identifiers is routed to the moderation portal for inspection.

Step 03

Network Blacklisting & Ban

Violators face immediate session termination, temporary suspension, or permanent IP/hardware bans.
